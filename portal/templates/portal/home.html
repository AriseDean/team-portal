How it works

  1. Register onceOne person fills in the team; everyone receives the Team ID by email.
  2. Keep your ID safeYou need it to submit. Typos can be fixed later with your edit PIN.
  3. Upload the reportAny teammate can upload. One report per team, so one upload covers everyone.

Good to know

I haven't registered my team yet

You'll need a Team ID before you can submit. Register first, it only takes a couple of minutes.

I lost my Team ID

Use Find my team. Enter your enrolment number and registered email and we'll send it again.

A teammate already uploaded the report

Then your team is done. When you enter your Team ID, you'll see who submitted and when.

I need more time

Ask your instructor. Extensions can be granted to a single team or to everyone.
